# SOP-010 Ambient Storage

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs ambient storage. It belongs to the Warehousing area. Document number SOP-010, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-010 applies to Hematology products. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for ambient storage.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-010. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-010 Ambient Storage, version 1, are:

- Storage range: 15 to 25 °C
- Relative humidity limit: 60 percent
- Warehouse mapping interval: 36 months

## Procedure

1. Store product only in mapped ambient zones.
2. Review temperature and humidity records weekly.
3. Quarantine product stored outside the stated range.
4. Repeat warehouse mapping at the stated interval.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-010 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
