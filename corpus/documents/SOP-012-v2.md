# SOP-012 Batch Release (version 2)

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs batch release. It belongs to the Quality Operations area. Document number SOP-012, version 2, status current.

## Scope

SOP-012 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for batch release.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-012. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-012 Batch Release, version 2, are:

- Retained samples per batch: 10 units
- Release decision due: 2 business days from completed review
- Retention period: shelf life plus 12 months

## Procedure

1. Confirm batch record review is complete.
2. Confirm all linked deviations are closed or assessed as not affecting release.
3. Take retained samples in the stated quantity.
4. Record the release decision.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-012 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
