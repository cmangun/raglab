# SOP-003 Change Control (version 1)

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs change control. It belongs to the Quality Systems area. Document number SOP-003, version 1, status superseded.

## Scope

SOP-003 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for change control.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-003. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-003 Change Control, version 1, are:

- Impact assessment due: 5 business days from submission
- Approvers required: 3

## Procedure

1. Submit the change request with the reason and scope.
2. Complete the impact assessment within the stated time.
3. Obtain approvals before implementation.
4. Verify effectiveness after implementation and close the record.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-003 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
