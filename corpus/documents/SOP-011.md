# SOP-011 Batch Record Review

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs batch record review. It belongs to the Quality Operations area. Document number SOP-011, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-011 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for batch record review.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-011. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-011 Batch Record Review, version 1, are:

- Review due: 5 business days from batch completion
- Second-person verification: required for all critical steps

## Procedure

1. Confirm every entry is complete, legible and signed.
2. Confirm deviations are recorded and assessed.
3. Return incomplete records to manufacturing.
4. Forward reviewed records for batch release.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-011 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
