# SOP-013 Equipment Calibration

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs equipment calibration. It belongs to the Engineering area. Document number SOP-013, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-013 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for equipment calibration.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-013. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-013 Equipment Calibration, version 1, are:

- Calibration interval for balances and pipettes: 6 months
- Out-of-tolerance report due: 1 business day
- Calibration label: required on every instrument

## Procedure

1. Calibrate against traceable standards.
2. Label the instrument with the calibration and due dates.
3. Report out-of-tolerance results within the stated time.
4. Assess the impact on batches made since the last passing calibration.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-013 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
