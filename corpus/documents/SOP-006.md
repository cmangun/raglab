# SOP-006 Hematology Control Filling

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs hematology control filling. It belongs to the Manufacturing area. Document number SOP-006, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-006 applies to Hematology products. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for hematology control filling.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-006. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-006 Hematology Control Filling, version 1, are:

- Fill volume tolerance: plus or minus 2 percent
- Line clearance check interval: 4 hours
- Cell suspension mixing speed: 60 rpm

## Procedure

1. Verify line clearance before the first fill.
2. Check fill volume at the start of each run and at the stated interval.
3. Keep the cell suspension mixing throughout filling.
4. Reconcile labels at the end of the run.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-006 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
