# SOP-015 Supplier Qualification

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs supplier qualification. It belongs to the Supply Chain area. Document number SOP-015, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-015 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for supplier qualification.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-015. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-015 Supplier Qualification, version 1, are:

- Requalification interval: 36 months
- On-site audit: required for critical suppliers
- Certificate of analysis: required with every lot

## Procedure

1. Classify the supplier as critical or non-critical.
2. Qualify the supplier before first purchase.
3. Verify a certificate of analysis with every lot received.
4. Requalify at the stated interval.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-015 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
