# SOP-009 Frozen Storage

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs frozen storage. It belongs to the Warehousing area. Document number SOP-009, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-009 applies to Molecular products. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for frozen storage.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-009. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-009 Frozen Storage, version 1, are:

- Storage temperature: at or below -15 °C
- Cumulative excursion limit above -15 °C: 2 hours
- Freezer alarm test interval: 3 months

## Procedure

1. Store product only in qualified freezers.
2. Respond to freezer alarms within 30 minutes.
3. Quarantine product when the cumulative excursion limit is exceeded.
4. Test freezer alarms at the stated interval.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-009 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
