# SOP-016 Complaint Handling

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs complaint handling. It belongs to the Customer Quality area. Document number SOP-016, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-016 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for complaint handling.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-016. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-016 Complaint Handling, version 1, are:

- Acknowledgement due: 3 business days
- Investigation due: 30 calendar days
- Retained sample testing: performed when the complaint concerns product performance

## Procedure

1. Log the complaint on the day it is received.
2. Acknowledge within the stated time.
3. Test retained samples when relevant.
4. Close the complaint with the investigation result.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-016 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
