# SOP-002 Deviation Management

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs deviation management. It belongs to the Quality Systems area. Document number SOP-002, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-002 applies to all sites. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for deviation management.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-002. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-002 Deviation Management, version 1, are:

- Initial assessment due: 2 business days from discovery
- Closure target for major deviations: 30 calendar days
- Closure target for minor deviations: 45 calendar days

## Procedure

1. Record the deviation on the day it is discovered.
2. Classify severity as minor or major.
3. Investigate root cause in proportion to severity.
4. Raise a change control when a procedure, material or equipment change is needed.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-002 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
