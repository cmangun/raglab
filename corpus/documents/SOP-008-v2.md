# SOP-008 Cold Chain Storage (version 2)

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs cold chain storage. It belongs to the Warehousing area. Document number SOP-008, version 2, status current.

## Scope

SOP-008 applies to Clinical Chemistry and Immunoassay products. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for cold chain storage.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-008. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-008 Cold Chain Storage, version 2, are:

- Storage range: 2 to 8 °C
- Cumulative excursion limit above 8 °C: 6 hours
- Logger download interval: 24 hours

## Procedure

1. Store product only in mapped cold rooms.
2. Download temperature loggers at the stated interval.
3. Quarantine product when the cumulative excursion limit is exceeded.
4. Record every excursion as a deviation.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-008 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
