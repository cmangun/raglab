## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for environmental monitoring.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-014. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-014 Environmental Monitoring, version 1, are:

- Viable air sampling frequency: every 7 days
- Action limit for viable air samples: 100 CFU per cubic metre
- Template-free room surface testing: every 14 days

## Procedure

1. Sample at the mapped locations.
2. Incubate and read plates per the method.
3. Investigate any result above the action limit.
4. Trend results quarterly.
