# SOP-004 Reagent Bulk Hold

## Purpose

Synthetic document. Calder Ridge Diagnostics and everything described here are invented for a retrieval evaluation demo.

This procedure defines how Calder Ridge Diagnostics performs reagent bulk hold. It belongs to the Manufacturing area. Document number SOP-004, version 1, status current.

## Scope

SOP-004 applies to Clinical Chemistry products. It covers routine operation and does not cover validation studies.

## Definitions

Deviation: an unplanned departure from an approved procedure. Change control: the documented review and approval of a change before it is made. Process owner: the person accountable for reagent bulk hold.

## Responsibilities

The process owner maintains SOP-004. Operators follow it and record their work. Quality reviews the records and assesses any deviation under SOP-002.

## Parameters

The controlled parameters for SOP-004 Reagent Bulk Hold, version 1, are:

- Maximum bulk hold time before filling: 72 hours
- Hold temperature: 2 to 8 °C
- Mixing before filling: 15 minutes

## Procedure

1. Label the bulk vessel with batch, time of formulation and hold expiry.
2. Store the bulk in the designated cold room.
3. Mix before filling for the stated time.
4. Reject bulk that exceeds the maximum hold time.

## Records

Records generated under SOP-004 are kept with the batch record or the quality system file and are subject to SOP-001 Document Control.
